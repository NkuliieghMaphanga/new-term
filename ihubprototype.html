iHub Africa Website Prototype

Group Client Project • Sprint 5

The iHub Africa Website Prototype was developed as a collaborative capstone project during the Zaio × iHub Africa Full Stack Web Development Programme. The objective was to design and develop a modern, responsive website that represents iHub Africa's mission, programmes and impact while demonstrating professional teamwork, Git collaboration and front-end development best practices.

Project Overview

This project simulated a real client engagement where our team was tasked with designing and developing a functional website prototype for iHub Africa.

Our solution focused on creating an intuitive user experience that effectively communicates iHub Africa's mission, training programmes, services and community impact while maintaining a modern, responsive interface.

Throughout the project we applied Agile collaboration, Git version control, responsive web design principles and semantic HTML to deliver a professional website prototype.

Client Research

Before beginning the design and development process, our team researched iHub Africa to gain a clear understanding of the organisation's mission, services and target audience. This research helped us design a website that accurately represented the organisation's identity while creating an engaging user experience.

We explored the programmes offered by iHub Africa, analysed the existing online presence and identified the most important information prospective students and partners would need when visiting the website.
